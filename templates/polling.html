Stage 3: Polling Day

It's election day! Here is what you need to know and do when you arrive at the polling booth.

What to Bring

⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTICE

Mobile phones, cameras, or any other electronic gadgets are NOT allowed inside the polling booth.

  • Your Voter Information Slip (highly recommended as it speeds up the process).
  • An approved Identity Document. Even if you have the voter slip, an ID is mandatory. Valid IDs include:
    • EPIC (Voter ID Card)
    • Aadhaar Card
    • PAN Card
    • Driving License
    • Passport
    • Passbook with photograph issued by Bank/Post Office

Step-by-Step Voting Process

1

First Polling Officer

They will check your name on the electoral roll and verify your ID document.

2

Second Polling Officer

They will ink your left forefinger, take your signature or thumb impression on the register, and give you a signed voter's slip.

3

Third Polling Officer

They will collect your signed voter's slip and press the "Ballot" button on the Control Unit to activate the EVM in the voting compartment.

4

Casting Your Vote

Proceed to the Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) inside the voting compartment. Press the blue button against the symbol/name of the candidate of your choice. You will hear a loud beep sound.

5

VVPAT Verification

Check the VVPAT machine's transparent window. A slip with the candidate's serial number, name, and symbol will be visible for 7 seconds before it drops into the sealed box.
