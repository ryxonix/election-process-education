Stage 1: Registration

Voter registration is the first step to participating in the democratic process. In India, you must be enrolled in the Electoral Roll to cast your vote.

Eligibility

  • You must be an Indian citizen.
  • You must be 18 years of age or older on the qualifying date (usually January 1st of the year of revision of electoral roll).
  • You must be an ordinary resident of the polling area where you wish to enroll.

How to Register (Form 6)

To register for the first time, you need to fill out Form 6. You can do this:

  • Online: Visit the Voters' Services Portal (voters.eci.gov.in) or use the Voter Helpline App.
  • Offline: Submit a physical Form 6 to the Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) or Booth Level Officer (BLO) of your area.

Documents Required

You will need proof of age and proof of residence. Common documents include:

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport
  • Driving License
  • PAN Card (Age proof)
  • Latest Water / Telephone / Electricity / Gas Connection Bill (Residence proof)
