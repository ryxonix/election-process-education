Stage 2: Verification

Just having an EPIC (Voter ID) card is not enough. Your name must be on the Electoral Roll (voter list) for the booth where you intend to vote.

Why Verify?

Electoral rolls are updated periodically. Sometimes names are removed due to change of address, death, or administrative errors. Verifying ensures you won't be turned away on polling day.

How to Verify Your Name

Method 1: Online Portal

Visit the Electoral Search portal (electoralsearch.eci.gov.in) and search by your EPIC number or basic details.

Method 2: Mobile App

Download the 'Voter Helpline App' by ECI. Scan your EPIC barcode or enter the number to find your details.

Method 3: SMS

Send an SMS: ECI <space> EPIC Number to 1950.

Method 4: Helpline

Call the toll-free Voter Helpline number 1950.

Voter Information Slip

A few days before the election, Booth Level Officers (BLOs) distribute official Voter Information Slips to households. This slip contains your Polling Station Name, Part Number, and Serial Number in the electoral roll. Keep it safe!
